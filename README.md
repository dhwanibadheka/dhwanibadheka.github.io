# Dhwani Badheka: portfolio site

How to put this site online with GitHub Pages (free). It takes about 15 minutes.

## 1. Add your photos

Put these files in the `images` folder, using exactly these names (lowercase, .jpg):

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `images/headshot.jpg` | Your portrait. A vertical photo works best. |
| `images/events/event-1.jpg`, `event-2.jpg`, `event-3.jpg` | Photos from the summit and other events. The first one shows largest. |

Keep each photo under about 1 MB. You can shrink large photos at squoosh.app. Until a photo is added, the site shows a neat empty box in its place.

## 2. Add your LinkedIn and photo captions

Open `content.js`. Put your LinkedIn URL at the top, and change the photo captions if you like.

## 3. Put it on GitHub

1. Create a free account at github.com. Choose your username carefully: it becomes your web address.
2. Click **+** (top right), then **New repository**.
3. Name the repository exactly `YOURUSERNAME.github.io`, using your own username. Set it to **Public** and click **Create repository**.
4. Click **uploading an existing file**. Drag in **everything inside** this folder (`index.html`, `style.css`, `content.js`, the resume PDF, and the `images` folder). Drag in the files themselves, not the folder that contains them.
5. Click **Commit changes**.
6. Go to **Settings**, then **Pages**. Under "Branch," pick `main` and `/ (root)`, then click **Save**.
7. Wait 1 to 2 minutes, then visit `https://YOURUSERNAME.github.io`.

## 4. Updating later

* **Text, interviews, or links:** open the file on GitHub, click the pencil icon, edit, then click **Commit changes**. The site updates in about a minute.
* **New resume:** upload a new `DhwaniBadheka_Resume.pdf` with the same name. It replaces the old one.

## 5. Share it

* Add the URL to your resume header, next to LinkedIn.
* Add it to the Featured section and contact info on your LinkedIn profile, and to your email signature.
